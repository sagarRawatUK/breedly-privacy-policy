1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("Breedly", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Breedly mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

Breedly is designed to help pet owners, breeders, and other members of the pet community discover compatible pets, manage pet profiles, review health information, send requests, communicate with other users, and plan potential pairings.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, password or authentication information, and profile information.
  • Pet information: pet name, species, breed, sex, age, photographs, pedigree or registration information, temperament, measurements, and other information you choose to provide.
  • Health information: vaccination records, test results, certificates, veterinary information, health scores, genetic or screening results, and related documents you upload or enter.
  • Breeding and matching information: preferences, pairing requests, match-related information, breeding availability, and information about planned pairings.
  • Messages and content: messages, attachments, photographs, documents, and other content you send or upload through the Services.
  • Support information: information you provide when contacting us for help.

Information collected automatically

  • Device type, operating system, app version, language, and general device identifiers.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Usage information, such as screens viewed, features used, dates and times of activity, and error or diagnostic information.
  • Log and security information used to prevent abuse and protect the Services.

Information from other sources

We may receive information from service providers that help us authenticate accounts, process payments, provide analytics, send notifications, or otherwise operate the Services. Where permitted by law, we may also receive information that another Breedly user chooses to share with you or with us.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and maintain your account and pet profiles.
  • Provide discovery, matching, request, messaging, and pet-management features.
  • Calculate and display match scores and related compatibility information.
  • Store, organize, and display health, pedigree, registration, and other pet records that you choose to provide.
  • Facilitate communication between users and allow users to share information and documents.
  • Send service messages, notifications, security alerts, and other operational communications.
  • Personalize and improve the Services, including understanding which features are useful.
  • Detect fraud, spam, misuse, unauthorized access, and other security threats.
  • Comply with applicable law, legal process, and legitimate requests from authorities.
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We may share information in the following circumstances:

With other Breedly users

Information you place in a public or discoverable pet profile may be visible to other users as part of the matching and discovery experience. When you send or accept a request, certain profile information may become available to the other party. Messages and files you send are shared with their intended recipients.

With service providers

We may use vendors that provide hosting, cloud storage, authentication, analytics, communications, customer support, security, payments, and other infrastructure services. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide services to us and subject to appropriate contractual or legal protections.

For legal and safety reasons

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to legal process, protect users or the public, investigate suspected fraud or abuse, or protect our rights and property.

Business transfers

If Breedly or substantially all of its assets are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale, or similar transaction, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law.

5. Pet Health and Breeding Information

Breedly allows users to store and share information about their pets, including health records, genetic screening results, vaccination records, pedigree information, and breeding details. Some of this information may be sensitive in nature even though it relates to an animal rather than a person.

Please upload only information that you have the right to share. You are responsible for reviewing the accuracy of records you provide and for deciding which information to make available to other users.

Breedly does not provide veterinary diagnosis or medical advice. Match scores and health indicators are informational tools and should not replace advice from a qualified veterinarian or other appropriate professional.

6. Location Information

If you enable location features, Breedly may use your device's location or an approximate location to provide distance-based discovery and matching features. You can control location permissions through your device settings. Some features may be unavailable or less accurate if location access is disabled.

7. Messages and User Content

Messages, attachments, photographs, documents, and other content you submit through Breedly may be stored and processed so we can deliver, secure, moderate, and operate the messaging and matching features.

Do not send passwords, financial account credentials, government identification numbers, or other information that you do not want to share with the recipient. Users should exercise care when communicating with people they do not know.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations. Retention periods vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

When information is no longer required, we may delete it, anonymize it, or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable law and legitimate business requirements.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
